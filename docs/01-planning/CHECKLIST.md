# Trust build checklist

Tick items off as they are completed and link to the note or decision behind each.

## 1. Define goals
- [ ] Write down what the trust should accomplish (see [GOALS.md](GOALS.md))
- [ ] Decide on the trust type with the attorney (e.g. revocable living trust, irrevocable trust)
- [ ] Identify the state whose law will govern the trust

## 2. Choose the people
- [ ] Grantor(s) / settlor(s)
- [ ] Initial trustee(s)
- [ ] Successor trustee(s), with a backup
- [ ] Beneficiaries and contingent beneficiaries
- [ ] Guardian for minor children (usually named in a will, not the trust)

## 3. Inventory assets
- [ ] List all assets in [the asset inventory](../03-assets/ASSET_INVENTORY.md)
- [ ] Note current ownership/title for each asset
- [ ] Note existing beneficiary designations (retirement accounts, life insurance)

## 4. Draft and sign
- [ ] Engage an estate-planning attorney
- [ ] Review the draft trust agreement
- [ ] Pour-over will
- [ ] Durable financial power of attorney
- [ ] Advance healthcare directive / healthcare power of attorney
- [ ] Sign and notarize (per state requirements)
- [ ] Obtain a certification/abstract of trust for use with banks

## 5. Fund the trust
An unfunded trust does not control assets. For each asset:
- [ ] Real estate: record a new deed into the trust
- [ ] Bank and brokerage accounts: retitle or add the trust as owner
- [ ] Retirement accounts and life insurance: review beneficiary designations (usually not retitled; ask the attorney/tax advisor)
- [ ] Vehicles and personal property: assignment of personal property
- [ ] Business interests: assign per the operating agreement
- [ ] Update the inventory's "Funded?" column as each is completed

## 6. Store and share
- [ ] Store signed originals securely (fireproof safe / attorney's vault)
- [ ] Tell successor trustees where the documents are and how to reach the attorney
- [ ] Keep encrypted digital copies outside this repo

## 7. Maintain
- [ ] Schedule an annual review (see [administration](../07-administration/README.md))
- [ ] Revisit after major life events: marriage, divorce, birth, death, move to another state, major asset purchase or sale
